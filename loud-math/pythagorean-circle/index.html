The circle is Pythagoras

Every point on a circle is exactly r away from the center. That's the definition of a circle.

But "distance" means Pythagoras. A point at (x, y) is the tip of a right triangle with legs x and y, and hypotenuse r. So:

x² + y² = r²

That's literally the equation of a circle. Same thing.

Drag the point around the circle. Watch the blue square (x²) and the red square (y²) change shape. Their combined area always equals the gold square (r²). Always. That's the whole secret.

When r = 1, cosθ is just x and sinθ is just y. The unit circle is where trig comes from.
